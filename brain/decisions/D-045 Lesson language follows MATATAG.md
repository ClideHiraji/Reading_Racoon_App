# D-045 Lesson language follows MATATAG

**Decision:** Levels 1-2 are in **Bisaya** (the learners' first language in Butuan), Levels 3-4 in **Filipino**.
The **pre-test and post-test are fully in Filipino**, including their Level 1-2 and Level 5 items. Level 5 stays
**English**, as an adventure extension only.

**Why**
- MATATAG Grade 1 Reading and Literacy is taught in the learner's first language; Filipino and English start as
  subjects in Grade 2. ARAL KS1 teaches Filipino before English.
- An English item can be failed for not knowing English rather than for not reading, which weakens the pre/post
  results in Chapter 4.
- Bisaya first, then Filipino, is the mother-tongue bridge, and makes language part of the level progression.

**Consequences**
- Levels 1-2 are rewritten, not translated: Bisaya phonics works by syllables (pantig), in ARAL's letter order
  (m, s, a, i, o, b, u, then k, l, t, n, g, p). The English Level 1-2 items retire.
- Pictures must have one clear Bisaya or Filipino name.
- All Bisaya and Filipino lines need a teacher's check before the study.
- The tests stay one language, so a child's test score doesn't depend on switching languages mid-test.

**Rejected:** all Filipino (doesn't follow MATATAG's first-language rule); all Bisaya (the tests and Levels 3-4
are already Filipino, and Filipino is what ARAL uses); English at Levels 1-2 (measures English, not reading).
