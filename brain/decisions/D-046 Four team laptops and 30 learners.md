# D-046 Four team laptops and 30 learners

**Decision:** The adviser's instruction: both sections play at school on **4 Windows laptops the team provides**, not
on phones. The study takes **30 learners, 15 per section** (Advanced, Teacher Delgado; Regular, Teacher Estremos),
down from 62.

**Why**
- Parents and teachers don't want the children using phones during class.
- 4 laptops for 15 children per section is a group the team can supervise and the laptops can serve.

**Consequences**
- Each laptop is shared by several children: the game already keeps a separate profile and upload queue per child,
  and "Switch student" waits until that child's answers are uploaded.
- Signing in and switching need the server, so each laptop needs a working connection. Bring a pocket Wi-Fi or
  hotspot instead of relying on the school Wi-Fi.
- Bring a USB mouse (dragging and spinning on a touchpad is hard at 6) and kid-size headphones (4 laptops in one room)
  for each laptop. A team member signs the children in.
- Playing time drops to about a quarter of the class period per child per day (15 children, 4 laptops), with no
  after-school play at home. Over a 3-day pilot that may be 20-30 minutes of play, which may be too little to show a
  pre/post gain. Log the minutes each child played and discuss the pilot length with the adviser.
- The post-test opens on a fixed day for everyone; with this little time nobody finishes all 5 worlds.
- Statistics: paired test per section with n = 15; check normality (Shapiro-Wilk), Wilcoxon signed-rank if it fails.
- The Windows export is the build to test; the Android APK is no longer needed for the study.
- Manuscript: participants 62 → 30 (15 per section), how the 30 are picked, devices (team laptops instead of phones,
  and why), Table 3.8 device requirements, the deployment steps.

**Still open:** how the 30 are picked from the children with consent; whether children may still play at home on
phones after school; the pilot length; checking the Windows build and switching students on a slow connection on one
of the 4 laptops.

**Rejected:** the earlier plan (Advanced on 3 shared laptops, Regular on parents' phones, all 62 children), replaced
by the adviser's instruction.
