## Learned
- The game currently doesn't handle Android Back, so Godot's default quits it; no safe-area insets are applied; the layout is portrait-only, which matters on landscape laptops. *(Fixed later the same day, D-023.)*
- A `VBoxContainer` can't be turned sideways at runtime; a `BoxContainer` with `vertical` can. [[Godot boxes can't change orientation]]
- A container whose child is bigger than the screen grows past the screen's edges, centered, so the top gets cut off as well as the bottom. `FitBox` asks for no room itself and scales its child down instead.
