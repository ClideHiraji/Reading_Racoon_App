## Learned
- AI Studio's speech page still runs on the free tier without an API key, but stops after about 6 generations a day with "Quota limit reached". The API's free tier allows 10 requests a day for 3.8 Flash TTS.
- Gemini 3.8 TTS: `<long pause>` tags between sentences make clean cut points. 30–50 sentences per generation cut cleanly about 95% of the time, judged by each piece's length against its sentence.
- A lively voice draws out short exclamations ("Wow!"), so the length check needs more room for very short words.
- For one-second speech clips, MP3 beats Ogg Vorbis on size: each Vorbis file carries a ~4 KB header.
- [[Scratch copies lose files in Temp]]: robocopy keeps the files' old dates, and Windows' temp cleanup deleted 287 files from a scratch copy. Setting every copied file's date to now stops it.
- A click on AI Studio's "Link a paid API key" dialog can move it to its billing step. Close it with its own close button.

- AI Studio's free speech page isn't capped at 6 generations a day: the second session ran 15 (about 50 minutes of audio) with no "Quota limit reached".
- [[Cut TTS batches by aligning sentence lengths]]: cutting a batch at its longest pauses can shift every later clip by one line, and a loose length check won't notice when neighbouring lines are alike ("1 sa 4 ang tama!", "1 sa 5 ang tama!").
