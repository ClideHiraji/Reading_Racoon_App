## Learned
- Short exclamations recorded on their own fail. In a 15–18-line batch the model skips or shortens pauses, and the check rejects everything.
- Set them among 20–35 recorded lines of the same voice and language: [[Cut TTS batches by aligning sentence lengths]].
- AI Studio's free speech page allowed 20 generations in one quota day (midnight to midnight Pacific; 4 PM to 4 PM in the Philippines) without "Quota limit reached".

- **The editor keeps its own copy of `project.godot` and `export_presets.cfg`.** It saved its old copy of the export presets over edits made on disk. Edit them in the editor, or reload the project right after a file edit.
- **A threaded warm-up hiccup:** once in nine scratch runs (a screen-capture run), the start screen's background warm-up logged a parse error for `scenes/lock/lock_screen.tscn`. The file is fine; that run just skipped pre-loading it. It never happened in six plain runs.
