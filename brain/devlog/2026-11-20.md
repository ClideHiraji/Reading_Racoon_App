# 2026-11-20

## Done
- **Critique of the teacher dashboard and admin portal** (Impeccable, two independent reviewers on a demo database of 26 fictional learners): **20/40**, 4 P1 issues. The class page read like a report rather than a to-do list. It used BKT and game jargon. It failed basic accessibility: white on teal at 3.3:1, a 1.5:1 focus ring, no skip link, status shown by colour alone. Risky controls had no guard: one-click consent removal, unconfirmed Revoke and Deny, Remove on every row. Three pages scrolled sideways on a phone. Snapshot in `.impeccable/critique/`.

  - **Plain states** (Got it / Practising / Needs help / Not started), with P(L) numbers on request.
  - **Signature:** the class drawn on the five-world reading map.
  - **Learner list:** filter and sort; phones get cards.
  - **Learner page:** breadcrumb, previous/next, one small server-drawn chart per sub-skill, and a "Manage" section for risky actions with confirmations.
  - **Admin:** the overview leads with "Needs your attention" and shows failing privacy checks; the nav shows counts; Revoke states its consequence.
  - **Forms:** errors next to their field; the book form shows a few pages at a time; plain help on the video form.

The class page went from 158 KB, 90 forms and 112 buttons to 121 KB, 34 forms and 69 buttons. Its height went from 5,492 to 4,677 px at 1366×768. The phone top bar went from 144 to 92 px.

## Learned
- `php artisan serve` runs with OPcache (`revalidate_freq` 60). After editing a Blade view, the old compiled view can be served for up to a minute, even after `view:clear`. Wait a minute, or check the compiled markup, before judging a change.
- A `.sr-only` element inside a scrolling `.table-wrap` widens the whole page on a phone: it is positioned against the page, not the table. The fix is `position: relative` on the scroll container.
- With table rows turned into cards via `display: grid` on `td`, any cell holding several children breaks into stray rows. An absolutely positioned label (`td::before`) with left padding works whatever the cell holds.
- `impeccable detect` skips `.blade.php`, and the site's CSP blocks its overlay (script, then WebAssembly). Scan `.html` copies or rendered pages instead.

## Learned
- Short exclamations recorded on their own fail. In a 15–18-line batch the model skips or shortens pauses, and the check rejects everything.
- Set them among 20–35 recorded lines of the same voice and language: [[Cut TTS batches by aligning sentence lengths]].
- AI Studio's free speech page allowed 20 generations in one quota day (midnight to midnight Pacific; 4 PM to 4 PM in the Philippines) without "Quota limit reached".
