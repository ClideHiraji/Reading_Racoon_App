## Learned
- **A Blade directive glued to a word isn't compiled.** `got it@if` and `page@endsection` come out as raw text or a PHP parse error. Separate them with a tag or space, or pass the value as a double-quoted `@section` argument.
- **`@yield` escapes a section's string value,** so assert `can&#039;t` in tests. Also assert `assertDontSee('@section')`, so raw Blade can't pass a text check by accident.
- **`display: contents` on a parent link rule also catches an absolutely positioned overlay link.** It turned the map links into 0×0 boxes; check the rendered size of click targets.
- **A scrolling flex nav (`overflow-x: auto`) cuts off its children's focus rings.** Wrapping is safer than scrolling for a handful of links.
