## Done: visual bugs and the touch-scroll bug
- New `reading-racoon/tests/layout_audit.gd` (uncommitted): opens 70 screens and states (every section, a lesson item of each task type in its try-again and answer states, the stop card, rewards, each Closet tab, lock screens) and flags SPILL (a piece out of its card, tilts included), CUT (cut or split words), OFFSCREEN/NOTCH, OVERLAP and SCROLL (tappable cards that block drag-scroll), saving boxed screenshots and a report. Ran at 7 sizes and languages: phone 450x800 in en/fil/bis, a 400x902 phone with a notch, a 600x800 tablet, laptops 1366x768 and 1280x800.
- **Touch-scroll bug reproduced** on the Windows build with touch emulated from the mouse: a drag starting on a Library card doesn't move the list, one starting in the gap between cards does. Cause from Godot 4.7.2's source: buttons default to `MOUSE_FILTER_STOP`, so the press never reaches the ScrollContainer; with `MOUSE_FILTER_PASS` it does, and ScrollContainer's `NOTIFICATION_SCROLL_BEGIN` cancels the button's press. The learning path already does this (`world_trail.gd`), which is why only the map scrolls well. Affects Library, Practice, Watch, My Word Book and every Closet tab.
- **A crash on laptops:** `ui/fit_box.gd` (every lesson's task holder) can flip-flop between two scales when the task's minimum height depends on its width (wrapped labels): 0.78 and 0.98 alternate forever inside one layout pass, the deferred-call queue overflows and Godot crashes with signal 11 at `fit_box.gd:23`. Seen in 2 of 3 runs at 1366x768, on story items lc4-04, lc4-12 and lc5-07; not in 6 portrait runs.
- Other real layout bugs: story text squeezed into a narrow column beside its picture on laptops (`story_page.gd`, the margin isn't EXPAND_FILL); the answer sheet's title doesn't wrap, so long answers run off both sides; picture-choice cards with long labels ("a paper star") spill past the card and the screen on phones; a three-line answer card ("Climb the mountain again at night.") overflows on phones and laptops; the Bisaya reward card runs off the right edge on phones; Racoon's long Filipino bubble on Home and the map tucks its first line under the profile chip; Library titles on two lines push the language tag 10 px out of the card; Closet item cards are 7-9 px too narrow on every size; the laptop login's key grid and LET'S GO run off the sides at 1366x768 and 1280x800; Word Catch's falling words start above the screen (by design) and Rhyme Pop balloons are clipped at the bottom while rising.
