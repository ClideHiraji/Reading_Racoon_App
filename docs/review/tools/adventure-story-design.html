15 new recommendations NEW IDEA

What the research says: the fun has to come from the reading itself. A review of 309 phonics apps found only about 27% support reading; the weak ones work like games or flashcards where the fun has nothing to do with reading. Rewards help, but too many make children read for the prize.
#StyleHow it playsSkillEffort
1Feed the buddyHear a word, drag the matching picture-food into the buddy's mouth; the buddy chews and reactsSounds, wordsEasy
2Sound basketsSort falling pictures into the /m/ or /s/ basket by first soundSoundsEasy
3Syllable trainLink syllable cars ("ba" + "ka"); the train leaves only when the word is right. Fits ARAL's syllable stepPhonicsMedium
4Sound boxesHear "baka", drop one stone per syllable or sound (Elkonin boxes)SoundsEasy
5Magic wand swapChange one letter and the picture transforms (bata → baka)Phonics, harder tierMedium
6Piko (hopscotch)Hop onto the tile with the syllable you hear (Laro ng Lahi)PhonicsMedium
7Hidden picture huntBusy scene; hear "Asa ang baka?" and find itVocabularyEasy–medium
8BingoThe buddy calls a word, the child marks it; 3 in a row winsVocabularyMedium
9FishingFish carry words; catch the one you hear (new look for Word Catch)WordsEasy
10Word search, picture crosswordFor the Advanced section's English trackVocabularyMedium
11Read it, make it happenRead "Ang pusa sa ilalim sa lamesa", drag the cat under the tableComprehensionMedium
12What happens next?The story stops; pick the next picture, then it continuesComprehensionEasy
13Story detectiveA short mystery ("Who took the pencil?"); each clue is a sentenceComprehensionMedium–hard
14Echo readingRacoon reads, the child records and plays back. No speech recognition, works offline and in BisayaFluencyEasy–medium
15Grow a garden or petReading earns water; a plant or pet grows. Keep it lightMotivationMedium

Top 5 picks: Feed the buddy (1), Sound boxes (4), Syllable train (3), Read it, make it happen (11), Echo reading (14).
Not for now: automatic read-aloud checking (no offline Bisaya speech model), and any new style before the Dec 1–3 pilot.

Every style is a general type of game, not a copy of a specific existing game. Sources are mostly app roundups and small studies.

3. The story: "Pauli na ta!" (Let's go home!)

A storm carries Roco's (or Rina's) little bangka away from home, a bahay kubo at the foot of Mount Mayapay. The buddy wakes up far down the Agusan River. To get home, the child helps the buddy travel back up the map through the 5 worlds the game already has. Each world is one level, its 5 quests are the stops on the path, and each world has a light guardian who helps the buddy go on. A shadow of the next guardian shows on the map, so children wonder who comes next.

4. The Adventure opening (storyboard)

Plays once, the first time the child opens Adventure, before the pre-test. About 25 seconds, Bisaya, with a skip button. Later visits open the map normally with a "watch again" button. The logo opening stays as it is.

#ShotWhat we seeWhat is said
1Home (5 s)A sunny kubo below Mount Mayapay. Nanay waves from the window. The buddy plays in a little bangka at the dock."Ako si Roco! Ganahan kaayo ko sa among balay."
I'm Roco! I love our house.
2Clouds (4 s)The sky turns grey, wind blows, leaves fly.Nanay: "Roco, sulod na! Moabot ang ulan!"
Come in! The rain is coming!
3Storm (5 s)Rain pours, the rope snaps, the bangka drifts down the Agusan River with the buddy holding on."Waaah! Nanay!"
4Waking up (5 s)Morning on a strange riverbank. The bangka is stuck in the roots."Asa man ko? Layo na kaayo ang among balay..."
Where am I? Home is so far away...
5Kidlap appears (4 s)A small green light floats closer."Kidlap-kidlap! Ayaw kabalaka. Tabangan ta ka makapauli!"
Don't worry. I'll help you get home!
6The map (3 s)The camera pans up the dark path to Mount Mayapay, home at the top. Fades into the real path screen."Tabangi ko, ha? Pauli na ta!"
Will you help me? Let's go home!
  • Keep the storm gentle: wind, rain and drifting, nobody hurt or crying hard. Children should feel "I want to help", not fear.
  • Nanay gives a reason to care, and she appears again in the homecoming video.
  • Roco and Rina need two versions. Show the buddy small or from behind in wide shots; only the close-ups (shots 1, 4, 6) need both.

Homecoming video (end of Mount Mayapay)

Haribon lands at the kubo. Nanay runs out and hugs the buddy, who now wears all 5 charms. The 5 guardians' lights circle the house. Buddy: "Salamat sa imong tabang! Nakauli na ko!" (Thank you for your help! I'm home!)

6. Making the videos

  • Godot doesn't play MP4. It plays Ogg Theora (.ogv). Make the animations as MP4 in any tool, then convert with one free ffmpeg command. The Watch player already plays .ogv.
  • 7 new videos: the Adventure opening, one per guardian (5), and the homecoming.
  • Finish and skip share each guardian's video; only the voice line changes. That halves the work.
  • Guardian videos 5–8 s, about 1–2 MB; opening about 3–5 MB, at 720×1280. Skip button after the first view.
  • The voice lines are recorded MP3s, like the buddy's lines now.

7. Fun without addiction

Use theseAvoid these
  • A clear goal: getting home
  • A map that lights up
  • Curiosity about the next guardian
  • Praise for skill (mastery skip)
  • A buddy the child cares about
  • Charms that show progress
  • Losing a streak
  • Random prize boxes
  • Countdown timers
  • "Come back or you'll miss out" prompts
  • Ending a session on a cliffhanger
  • Rewards bigger than the reading

The class schedule, sleep lock and daily minutes already in the game stop long play. When time runs out, the day ends calmly.

8. Two problems to decide

1. The post-test can't wait for "home" in a 3-day pilot. Most children won't finish 5 worlds by Thursday. The post-test must open on Day 3 for everyone, as the "letter from home". The homecoming stays at the end of Mount Mayapay.
2. Timing. The pilot starts Tuesday, Dec 1. Building the story, videos and new styles before then isn't realistic, and changing the game during the pilot makes the Chapter 4 data harder to defend. Recommended: run the pilot on the current game, and build this for the defense version or a later round.
